# Project Statement: Student Performance Tracker

## 1. Problem Statement
Students often struggle to track their academic progress across multiple subjects, maintain consistent study schedules, and organize daily tasks efficiently. Without a centralized tracking tool, it becomes challenging to identify weak academic areas early, monitor daily study hours, and ensure timely completion of assignments.

## 2. Scope of the Project
The scope of this project includes building a Python-based command-line interface (CLI) application that allows students to:
- Register subjects along with their obtained marks.
- Log daily study hours and track completed study sessions.
- Compute overall grade averages and automatically flag subjects that fall below performance thresholds.
- Maintain a dedicated task list for academic assignments and exams.
- Generate comprehensive progress reports directly in the terminal.

*Out of Scope:* Graphical User Interface (GUI), persistent external database integration, and multi-user authentication.

## 3. Target Users
- University and college students looking for a simple tool to manage their study routines.
- Self-learners and high school students who want to track study time and stay accountable.
- Anyone looking for a lightweight terminal tracker for task and grade management.

## 4. High-Level Features
- **Subject & Grade Management:** Add, update, and validate subject scores.
- **Study Session Logging:** Record specific study hours and subjects reviewed.
- **Performance Analyzer:** Automatically calculate average scores and highlight weak academic areas (< 40 marks).
- **Academic Task Manager:** Create to-do lists, track pending items, and mark finished tasks.
- **Consolidated Progress Report:** View complete academic summaries with total study hours and task statuses in one output view.
